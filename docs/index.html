No Xcode. No account to create.

Get the latest release↗
  1. 1

    Download & move to Applications

    Open GitHub Releases. Choose arm64 for M-series Macs or x86_64 for Intel. Open the DMG or unzip, then drag Clipori.app into Applications.

    Apple Silicon arm64Intel x86_64
  2. 2

    Before the first launch

    Current releases are not notarized by Apple. After downloading from this project's Releases, run this in Terminal to remove the app's quarantine flag:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Clipori.app

  3. 3

    Open Clipori. Copy something.

    Clipori lives in your menu bar. Press ⇧⌘V to open the shelf. Copied text and images appear automatically.

    Enable double-click to paste

    Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and enable Clipori. Without this permission, click a card to copy and press ⌘V in the destination app.

    Connect your own AI

    In Clipori Settings → AI, select OpenAI or Claude format, enter your base URL, model and API key, then test and save. History and keyword search work without AI. Automatic classification is optional.

A FEW LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Is Clipori free?

The current version is free and open source under Apache 2.0. AI uses the service you configure, which may charge separately.

How long is my history kept?

New installs keep 30 days by default. Choose 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 1 year, or forever. Pinned entries and Pinboards are kept until you remove them.

Does it sync with my iPhone?

This release is for Mac. An iPhone companion and iCloud sync are in development; cross-device sync is not ready for everyday use yet.

Can I save screenshots too?

Yes. Use ⌃⇧⌘4 to copy a screenshot selection to your clipboard. Clipori captures it automatically; press Space to preview. Screenshots saved only as files are not captured.
